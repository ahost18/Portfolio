PROCESSING OF TRADES

Outright and Direct-to-Store Accounting Procedures

1. OUTRIGHT BILLINGS

Outright billings cover products delivered directly to the warehouse.

Countering Outright Documents

  • Confirm that the accounting stamp is current.
  • Check that all required attachments are included: the original and copy of the Goods Receipt Note (GRN), and the original and copy of the Sales Invoice or Charge Invoice.
  • Check vendor-specific requirements before accepting documents.
  • Confirm that the GRN has been stamped by Receiving. For frozen products, the Sales Invoice must carry the Frozen Receiving stamp.
  • Stamp and sign both the original and copy of the Sales Invoice or Charge Invoice.
  • Collect the pink GRN and the original Sales Invoice. Confirm that the Sales Invoice is clearly marked “Original” or "Customer Copy"
  • Return the remaining documents to the vendor.

Vendor-Specific Reminders

  • Vendor A: Require the original and copy of the GRN, the original invoice, and the pink copy. Reject the documents if the pink copy is missing
  • Vendor B: Make sure the customer copy of the Sales Invoice is included; this is the copy to collect.
  • Vendor C: Always collect the original copy.
  • For Vendor D, verify the trucking company named on the Sales Invoice. Return invoices only through the same trucking company that submitted

Important: Some Sales Invoices contain multiple pages or deliveries from different trucks but refer to a single GRN.

Cross-check the total items on the GRN against the Sales Invoice. Take extra care when processing documents from vendors with special handling requirements

Outright Process

  • The vendor delivers the goods to the warehouse and issues a Sales Invoice.
  • Receiving stamps the Sales Invoice and provides a copy of the GRN.
  • Submit the complete documents to Accounting for billing.

The Receiving stamp is required before Accounting processes the documents because it serves as evidence that the goods were delivered to the warehouse

2. OUTRIGHT COSTING

  • Compare the total Sales Invoice amount with the GRN amount.
  • If the GRN amount is higher by 10 or more, write “DM = [difference amount]” on the GRN, circle the Sales Invoice amount, and sign.
  • If the Sales Invoice amount is higher, write the GRN amount on the Sales Invoice.

Discounts

If a discount is shown beside the vendor’s name, record the discount and subtract it from the total amount.

Multiple Invoices

Use the applicable quantity, unit of measure (UOM), net price, and total to calculate the amount. Apply VAT where

required by the original procedure, and reconcile the combined invoice amounts against the GRN.

  • For the specified vendor only, the notes state that excise tax depends on the type of sweetener used per liter.
  • The notes list a 6% tax for sugar, stevia, aspartame, or other non-caloric sweeteners, and ■12 per liter for high fructose drinks
  • The notes state that chocolate bricks, powdered milk, and ready-to-drink milk are exempt. Confirm current tax treatment before applying these notes.

3. PLOTTING AND TRANSMITTALS

  • Group Outright documents by payment terms, as shown on the GRN.
  • Combine documents with the same terms (for example, all 15-day terms).
  • Record the GRN number, Sales Invoice number, and total amount due.
  • Handle the specified “Q” terms separately.
  • Complete DSA and Outright processing by Monday and Wednesday night. Transmittals are scheduled for Tuesday and Thursday

Final Checks

  • For vendors that are not VAT-registered, add VAT only when the products are VATable and the procedure requires it
  • The source notes identify two vendors as subject to expanded withholding tax (EWT).
  • Some vendors deduct EWT upfront; always verify the deduction.
  • Do not stamp documents unless all required copies are present.
  • For the vendor with color-coded copies, stamp the pink copy rather than the green copy.

4. DIRECT-TO-STORE (DSA) BILLINGS

DSA billings cover stock delivered directly to stores.

Required Attachments

  • Invoice
  • Delivery Confirmation Report (DCR) with the store stamp
  • GRN

The source notes identify one vendor whose documents are costed in SAP.

DSA Costing

  • Compare the DCR amount with the Sales Invoice (SI).
  • If the DCR is lower than the SI by 10 or more, write the difference on the SI, circle the amount, and sign.
  • If the DCR is equal to or higher than the SI, circle the SI amount and sign. Add “DM = [difference amount]” when applicable
  • For multiple DCRs, match all listed items to the SI, add the DCR amounts, and compare the combined total with the SI

Checking Amounts in SAP

  • Log in to the production server and open transaction FBL1N.
  • Enter the vendor code shown on the GRN, or search by vendor name.
  • Enter company code 0311 (ATP), then select Open Items and execute.
  • After the results appear, select More and then Change Layout.
  • Add the Reference column. The reference is the GRN number shown on the attachment; the source example uses a suffix such as “_5006”.
  • Use Find or Filter to locate the relevant item, then open the amount.
  • Compare the “CD Base” amount with the GRN amount and record the appropriate amount on the documents.

Follow the applicable difference threshold and DM notation described in the costing procedure.

DSA and Outright Comparison

DSA also has monitoring records in Google Drive. The costing process is generally similar to Outright. However,

when there are multiple pages or a variance, the document layout may differ: the SI is at the front for DSA, while it

may appear on the second page for Outright. After completing the comparison, write the costed amount on the SI.
